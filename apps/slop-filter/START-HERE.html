Your feed.
Your personal tells.

Fold LinkedIn posts with the writing patterns you personally can’t stand. No account. No API key. Everything runs in your browser.

Install in Chrome · about a minute
  1. Unzip the download and keep this folder somewhere permanent.
  2. Type chrome://extensions in Chrome’s address bar.
  3. Turn on Developer mode in the top right.
  4. Choose Load unpacked. Select the included slop-filter folder containing manifest.json.
  5. Pin Slop Filter from Chrome’s Extensions menu. Refresh your LinkedIn tab.

Pick your icks.

Click the extension icon. Pick the tells you want. One match folds a post. Add your own phrases and click Save phrases.

More room for posts.

Under Feed layout, turn on Hide left sidebar or Hide right sidebar. Choices are remembered locally. Turn them off to restore the columns. Works on recognized desktop feed layouts; the mobile app is unaffected.

You’re in control.

One Flagged: line explains the match. Hover for the full reason. The eye reveals the post; eye-off collapses it. The checkmark keeps it visible for this visit, and the heart always shows that author. Hover over an icon for its label. Switch filtering off to restore the feed immediately. Slop Magnet reverses the filter if you’re feeling brave.

Same icks? Send a preset.

Click Export preset and send the downloaded JSON to a friend with the extension. They use Import preset, preview the tells and phrases, then apply. Try the included balanced.preset.json as an example.

To share the extension itself, send the original friend ZIP. This beta uses Developer mode; keep it enabled. A Chrome Web Store release is a separate future step for one-click installs and updates.

Personal style matching, not proof of AI authorship. Rules initially target English; custom phrases support other languages. Unknown layouts stay visible.
